# 01 · Positioning & Market 【提案】

## Positioning (one line)

**The closed-loop copilot for freelancers: from job post to paid invoice.**
Not another proposal generator — the only tool that follows the gig through win → contract →
milestones → payment chasing.

## Why now

1. Proposal-writing fatigue is the #1 time sink cited by Upwork freelancers; win rates on
   generic copy-paste proposals have collapsed as AI made everyone's proposals sound alike —
   *tailored* proposals now win, and tailoring at scale needs tooling.
2. Existing tools are single-point (proposal generators, invoice makers, contract templates).
   Nobody connects them; the handoffs are where freelancers actually lose money
   (no contract, no milestone reminders, no polite-but-firm late payment follow-ups).
3. 2026 saturated-list guidance says generic "AI writing tools" are dead; a workflow-embedded
   agent for a specific profession is the counter-play.

## Target users

| Priority | User | Pain | Willingness to pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Dev/design freelancers on Upwork, $3-15k/gig | 2-4 hrs/wk on proposals+admin; late payments | High — direct ROI on win-rate & cash flow |
| P1 | Agency-of-one consultants (Toptal, direct clients) | Contract & invoice hygiene | High |
| P2 | Newcomers trying to break in | Low win rate, no portfolio positioning | Low (free tier hook) |

## Competitive landscape (verified 2026-10)

| Competitor | What it does | Gap we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| Upwork's built-in AI proposal hints | One-shot suggestion inside job page | Not tailored to YOUR portfolio; no lifecycle |
| Generic proposal generators (many, incl. dead "AI business idea validators" cohort) | Template + LLM fill | Sound-alike output; nothing after submit |
| Bonsai / HelloBonsai | Contracts + invoicing suite for freelancers | No AI capture/tailoring; forms, not an agent; $25/mo anchor |
| Wave / Zoho Invoice | Invoicing | No gig context, no chasing agent |
| Boosted proposal tools (chrome extensions) | Job alerts, templates | No closed loop; low polish |

**Open lane**: the *loop* (capture → tailor → contract → milestones → chase) owned by one agent,
with the freelancer's portfolio as the personalization substrate. Bonsai is the closest neighbor
on the back half; nobody credible owns the front half + loop.

## Distribution

1. Build in public on X/LinkedIn (English) — the "solo dev ships gigflow" arc;
2. r/Upwork, r/freelance — help-first answers, tool in signature (beware self-promo rules);
3. Upwork community forums; Product Hunt launch at v0.1;
4. Content: "we analyzed 200 winning proposals" (from anonymized opt-in data, later).

## Kill criteria

- v0.1 live + 6 weeks: <10 freelancers using it for ≥2 real job applications → freeze;
- 12 weeks: <3 paying subscribers → freeze, postmortem to BLOG (English + Chinese versions).
